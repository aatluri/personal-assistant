Changed from the agreed mockup

  • Day view shows the last 7 days as daily bars, with the selected day highlighted. Steps are stored once per day, so hourly bars aren't possible.
  • "Data through" shows the latest date that has data, without a time.
  • Highlights use only stored data. "Longest run 8.2 km" replaces "New best 5k", because workouts have no split times.
  • Nutrition shows logged days instead of meals. Nutrition is stored as one total per day.
  • Recovery in Day view is labelled "today", because the values are daily, not morning readings.
  • In Week and Month views, tap a bar to open that day. The arrows step through days in Day view.
  • Icons come from the set the app already uses.

Decided

  • A night of sleep belongs to the day you woke up, as Health Auto Export stores it: a night from Nov 5 23:40 to Nov 6 06:52 shows under Nov 6. Overview and the Sleep log page use the same date.
  • Goals: 10,000 steps, 140 g protein, 7 hours of sleep, 3 litres of water.
